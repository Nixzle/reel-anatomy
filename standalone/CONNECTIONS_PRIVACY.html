Account connections: privacy and terms

Reel Anatomy is the Nixzle/reel-anatomy research and production application. This optional connection layer is distinct from accounts connected to ChatGPT. Updated 24 October 2026.

What is contacted, and when

The ordinary local library does not contact Google. Selecting “Load Google sign-in” loads Google's official Identity Services script directly from Google. Google can see the request and network address. YouTube and Drive authorisation are requested separately, only after an explicit action.

YouTube data

The read-only connector requests your accessible channel identities, upload metadata and selected YouTube Analytics reports. It cannot publish, change playlists, download videos or inspect another user's private analytics. Returned titles, counts and reports are shown only in this active session. They are not added to the research database, backups, encryption snapshots, assistant prompts, machine-learning training or custom viral scores. Missing values are not replaced with estimated values.

Account tokens are kept in memory, not browser storage, URLs or export files. YouTube responses are cleared on disconnect, token expiration, revocation or page exit. No server owned by this application receives your YouTube access token or reports. Google processes API and authorisation requests under its own policies.

By connecting YouTube, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See Google's privacy policy and manage or revoke Google account permissions. The app's Revoke button asks Google to revoke its grants and clears local session reports. Without a live token, use Google's account-permissions page. This app stores no persistent YouTube report copy to delete.

Encrypted metadata synchronisation

Drive access is limited to the application's hidden appDataFolder, not your ordinary Drive files. The app encrypts an approved metadata checkpoint on your device using AES-GCM and an authenticated index, then uploads only encrypted contents. Metadata includes every research project in the current device library and its saved sources, scripts, analyses, claims, creative observations and outcomes. Original video, audio, PDF files, downloaded model caches and YouTube account-session reports are excluded.

Google can see the existence of the files, opaque identifiers, sizes, timestamps and request metadata. The recovery key is not uploaded with the encrypted checkpoint. An encryption key is retained in this browser's IndexedDB separately from library backups. Drive's opaque account identifier is retained with the vault to reject a different Google account; the display name is session-only and is cleared when that session ends. A private recovery-key download is required for a second device. Losing the key may make cloud contents unrecoverable. A compromised browser or device, or any script with access to the same web origin, can still access decrypted research and use stored keys. Different paths on one GitHub Pages origin are not separate security boundaries; a dedicated origin is recommended before broad account-enabled deployment. This is not a claim of protection against a compromised endpoint.

Sync requires the same registered app client and authorising Google account on both devices. It is a reviewed foreground operation, not a continuously running background service. Conflicting records require explicit choices. Application permissions and human editorial approvals are not propagated as fresh authority. A pre-restore metadata copy is retained locally before applying a merge.

Retention and deletion

Encrypted checkpoints remain in your app-specific Drive storage until you remove that app's data through Drive settings. The client does not silently delete old cloud history. It stops at a bounded history limit. “Forget this device's vault key” removes that local key and base, not your local research or cloud files. Revoking Google permissions stops future API access but does not itself delete your encrypted files. Use Drive settings and Manage apps to remove the app's stored data. Save independent project-pack and recovery-key backups before deletion.

Browser storage contains the local research, the configured public OAuth client ID and any chosen encryption key/base. It can be cleared or evicted. The app does not sell this information, use it for advertising, send it to a language-model provider through this connection layer, or permit unrelated human access. Account reports are session-only; cloud research transfer happens only after explicit approval.

Setup, qualification and contact

The operator must register the correct Google OAuth web client, configure the allowed origins and APIs, and satisfy Google's consent/verification requirements before broad distribution. Tests of protocol fixtures are not proof of live account approval or a security certification. No account is preconnected in this release.

For questions or deletion guidance, contact the project maintainer through the project's issue tracker. Do not include tokens, recovery keys or private library content in a public issue. This page describes the application's data handling, not independent legal advice.
